## Why

Closes #1938, the second slice of #1042 (a texture tiles behind a surface, and a subtree fades out along a gradient),
which is a sub-issue of #203. Slice 1 (#1937, PR #1950) gave a box its tiles. Nothing in the vocabulary fades a
subtree: `BoxStyle.Opacity` is one alpha for the whole group, and the box's `Gradient` and `Glow` paint colour, they
do not take alpha away from what is drawn.

The demand is Cura's public page. Its azulejo, a 58 px tile of two lines and five circles, sits behind the hero's
profile and in a corner of three dark bands, and fades out along a radial gradient. Cura writes it in CSS keyed on its
sections' ids: a `::before` with `isolation: isolate` on its section, negative offsets past the section's edges,
`z-index: -1`, seven gradient layers and a radial `mask-image`.

The general capability is **a subtree fades along a linear or radial gradient of opacities**, on both targets, without
changing its layout or where it takes the pointer, and **a decoration may reach past the surface it sits on**, painted
behind that surface's content and above its background, clipped when the surface clips.

Flutter answers the fade with `ShaderMask(shaderCallback, blendMode: BlendMode.dstIn, child)`, a question about the
RenderObject tree (the third tree: how a subtree composites). It answers the overhang with `Stack(clipBehavior:
Clip.none)` and a `Positioned` at negative offsets, which our `Stack` already is.

**Blocked by #1952** (a Bug): Photon's live windows skip the offscreen layer phase, so a group opacity double-blends
there and a mask cannot draw. #1952 runs that phase on every present path and pools the layer targets. Until it merges,
this change's fade is proven on the offscreen path, and one task proves it on a live window after it. Its other
neighbours: #1953 (the blending space), #1954 (an overhang over a stack's neighbours on the web), #1955 and #1956 (the
box gradient's diagonal and its interpolation).

## What Changes

- **New** `Masked(Mask mask, VisualNode child)`: a single-child node that composites its child as one group and
  multiplies it by the mask's opacity at every point. It is layout-transparent and changes no hit test: a point the
  mask hides still takes the press it took before. Its factory is `Masked(mask, child)`.
- **New** `Mask`, a closed set with a visitor (`IMaskVisitor<TState, TResult>`), so a kind a realizer does not draw is
  a build error, as slice 1's `Pattern` is:
  - `LinearMask(float angle, IReadOnlyList<MaskStop> stops)`: the angle in degrees clockwise from "toward the top",
    the gradient line through the box's centre and long enough for its ends to touch the corners, as CSS and every
    design tool define it;
  - `RadialMask(Point center, RadialExtent extent, IReadOnlyList<MaskStop> stops)`: the centre as fractions of the
    box (the rule `Pattern.Anchor` and `Image.FocalPoint` follow), and an elliptical extent;
  - `MaskStop(float offset, float opacity)`: an opacity, 0 to 1, at a fraction of the gradient's ray. Any number of
    stops, one or more, resolved as CSS resolves them (measured in Chrome and Firefox): an offset below an earlier
    one moves up to it, repeated offsets make a hard step, offsets outside 0..1 are kept, and one stop is a solid
    opacity;
  - `RadialExtent`: a readonly record struct, either `Fraction(x, y)` of the box, `Dp(x, y)`, or one of the four
    sizes CSS names (`ClosestSide`, `FarthestSide`, `ClosestCorner`, `FarthestCorner`, the last the default).

  ```csharp
  Masked(new RadialMask(new(0.7f, 0.35f), RadialExtent.Fraction(0.7f, 0.7f), [new(0.35f, 1), new(0.76f, 0)]),
      Box(new BoxStyle { Background = Field, CornerRadius = new(28), Patterns = [Azulejo.Tile] }))
  ```

- **The web** lowers `Masked` to one wrapper element carrying `mask-image`, with the resolved stops written as CSS
  writes a gradient (`radial-gradient(70% 70% at 70% 35%, rgb(0 0 0 / 1) 35%, rgb(0 0 0 / 0) 76%)`), from SSR and the
  twin byte for byte.
- **Photon** draws the subtree into the group layer `BeginLayer` already opens, given a mask in the slots it
  leaves free, and composites it through a new shader entry `masked_layer_composite` (`Sdf.slang`, regenerated by
  `scripts/generate-shaders.sh`), which evaluates the mask at every device pixel in the node's local space. The stops
  are read texel by texel, with no filter, from a stop texture of a new data format, `TextureFormat.R32Float`, so a
  hard stop is as hard as the browsers draw it. The stop texture is cached per host by the resolved stops' value. The
  Reference backend is normative.
- **The overhang needs no new member.** A `Positioned` at negative offsets, the stack's first child, already paints
  past the stack, behind the later children and above the surface's background, and a surface with `Clip` clips it,
  on both targets. This change pins it with a test on each and writes it down. Measured, and left to its own issue:
  on the web an unclipped overhang paints over the stack's neighbours and takes their pointer, where Photon paints in
  tree order.
- **Samples:** Cura's azulejo, written in C# as a `DrawingPattern` over a 58 × 58 `VectorDrawing` and a `Masked`
  around a `Positioned` box, on a new `/azulejo` page of `samples/DefaultUIDashboard` and the same screen in
  `samples/PhotonDesktop`.
- **The cross-target golden:** one Reference-backend image per scene is the golden. Photon matches it as every golden
  does, and a new CI step opens the sample's page in headless Chrome and compares the browser's pixels with the same
  image, within tolerances measured here (a local Playwright spec does the same in Firefox). The fade is compared on
  its alpha, and the hero's azulejo on its colours, within 4/255 (Photon's linear-light blending puts it at most
  3/255 from the browsers', computed). Cura's dark slabs, 51 to 56/255 apart for the same reason, are held by each
  target's own golden and fenced from the comparison, pointing at #1953.

No break. `BoxStyle`, `Pattern` and the existing gradients keep their shape.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `box-paint`: a subtree fades along a mask of any number of stops, on both targets, with no change to layout or
  hit-testing; the hero's azulejo is held by one golden both targets are compared with.
- `layout`: a `Positioned` past its stack paints behind the stack's later children and above its surface, and a
  clipping surface clips it, on both targets.

## Impact

- **Vocabulary** (`eQuantic.UI.Primitives`): `Masked`, `Mask`, `LinearMask`, `RadialMask`, `MaskStop`,
  `RadialExtent`, `RadialExtentKind`, `IMaskVisitor`; `IVisualNodeVisitor` gains `Visit(Masked, …)`. The factory in
  `eQuantic.UI.Components`' `UI`. `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt` of Primitives and Components grow; nothing is removed.
- **The web realizer** (`eQuantic.UI.Web`): `MaskCss : IMaskVisitor`, the wrapper, `HtmlStyle.MaskImage`, the
  wrapper lists (`Fills`, `CapsAt`, …). Its `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt` grows by `HtmlStyle.MaskImage`.
- **The runtime twin** (`eQuantic.UI.Runtime`): the vocabulary classes, `lowerMasked` and the CSS writer, with the
  exhaustive `never` check.
- **Photon** (`eQuantic.UI.Native.Engine`, `.Reference`, `.Metal`, `.Vulkan`, `.Components`, `.Framework`):
  `TextureFormat.R32Float` and `TextureData.Floats` (and the Metal and Vulkan formats), `LayerMask`,
  `DisplayListBuilder.PushMaskLayer`, the pipeline and the shader entry, `MaskStopCache`, `EmitVisitor`,
  `SemanticsVisitor` and `MeasureVisitor`. The engine's `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt` grows (experimental surface); nothing
  is removed. The live window paths are not touched: #1952's.
- **Samples and CI:** `Azulejo.cs` and an `/azulejo` page in `samples/DefaultUIDashboard`, linked into
  `samples/PhotonDesktop`; `scripts/check-cross-target-goldens.cs`, a new step of CI's `samples` job.
- **Not reached:** eqc (a vocabulary record needs no rule of its own), the SDKs, the templates, the developer surface
  (`developer-surface.baseline.txt` does not move), the email realizer (it lowers a `Masked` as its child: a mail
  client has no mask, and the email realizer's own fence list names it).
- **Principles followed:** 4 (written once, realized per target), 5 (no web word: `mask-image` is the web's, the node
  is `Masked`), 6 (Flutter's `ShaderMask`, and wider: an elliptical extent and CSS's sizes), 9 (the structural fix: an
  exact data texture rather than an approximating ramp, and MSBuild's linked file for the one source both samples
  share), 13 (nothing kept), 15 (a capability is a node around any child, never a flag on `Box`). **Bent:** none.
