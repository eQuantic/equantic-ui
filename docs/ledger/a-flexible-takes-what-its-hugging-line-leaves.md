- **2026-11-27 · A Flexible takes what its hugging line leaves, and the Studio's search field stays in its cell**: on a
  line its content sizes, Photon offered a Flexible's max-content probe the line's whole room, so a text entry between
  its icon and its clear button took it all. The Studio's search field ran 88dp into the next grid column, whose stepper
  took the press meant for the clear button. The probe is now offered what the line has left, and flexibles that
  overrun it together shrink as a browser shrinks them, which puts the entry at 192dp, as the web lays it out
  ([#1256](https://github.com/eQuantic/equantic-ui/issues/1256)). Proposed and archived through OpenSpec
  (`openspec/specs/flex-layout`).
