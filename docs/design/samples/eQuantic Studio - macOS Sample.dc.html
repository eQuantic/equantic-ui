On macOS the mobile BottomSheet becomes a popover anchored to its trigger; ActionSheet becomes a context menu; Toast docks bottom-trailing. Modal keeps the same anatomy, centered on the window with E4.
Hover states exist here (Layer 2 desktop adapter): every pressable surface gets a SurfaceSubtle hover fill 100ms before its pressed state.
